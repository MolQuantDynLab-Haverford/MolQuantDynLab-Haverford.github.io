title: "Collaborative paper on ketene-He collisions has been published in JCP."

A collaborative work led by Abdelhak Jrad and Manel Naouai (University of Tunis El Manar, Tunisia) on the inelastic scattering of ketene (H<sub>2</sub>CCO) induced by collisions with He atoms, including a non-LTE analysis, has been published in the Journal of Chemical Physics.
